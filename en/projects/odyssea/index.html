Project 03

Odyssea

Rock opera band · “Road to Olympus” tour · group project

End-of-year final project: as a group, create a band and its international tour, with a music genre drawn at random. Ours: rock opera.

We imagined Odyssea, a legendary band ready for its big comeback with a world tour, “Road to Olympus”.

01

Moodboard

Ancient Greece meets rock: pottery, temples, flames and electric guitars.

02

Brandboard

A Spartan helmet struck by lightning, three typefaces and a textured black that lets the orange burst out.

03

Campaign

Posters, flyers, tickets, a costume photo shoot, a filmed interview and a website: the tour takes shape on paper, in photos and on video.
