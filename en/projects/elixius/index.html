Project 02

Elixius

Perfume collection · group project

For an end-of-year project, the set theme was “disruptive fragrance”.

As a group, we created Elixius, a luxury perfume house built on two opposite sensations: the intense heat of Éruption Solaire and the extreme cold of Zéro Absolu.

01

Moodboard

Fire against ice: volcanoes and sunsets facing frost, northern lights and deep blues.

02

Brandboard

A bottle-shaped monogram, a Didot typeface and two colour worlds.

03

Campaign

“Collection Contraste”: one poster per sensation, then both bottles together.
