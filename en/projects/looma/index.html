Project 01

Looma

Eco-friendly clothing brand · 2024

Looma is a local brand of harem trousers made from durable, biodegradable materials, designed as an alternative to fast fashion.

The intent: a soft, natural identity that makes people want to dress comfortably while doing something good for the planet.

Morning light, crumpled linen, wood, plants and earthy tones: a cocooning mood.

03

Campaign

“Ethical comfort for an authentic style”: LinkedIn banner, Instagram page and outdoor posters.
