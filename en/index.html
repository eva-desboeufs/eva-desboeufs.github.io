Method

My process

I start from a mood, turn it into an identity, then into a campaign.

  1. Moodboard

    Images, light and colour: I set the mood before drawing anything.

  2. Brandboard

    Logo, variations, palette, typefaces and patterns: the mood becomes a system.

  3. Campaign

    Posters, social media, printed items: the identity meets its audience.
